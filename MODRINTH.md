- Per-channel formatting (Public / Party / Guild / Officer) — custom prefixes, colors and rank display

- **Custom Rules** — highlight matching text or hide messages, with channel selection and optional case sensitivity
- Custom highlight colors, bold, italic and underline
- Ctrl+F search, right-click copy and extended history
- Compact Pickup Stash reminders with a working `[PICK UP]` button and grouped pickup summaries
- Player muting with `/hsc mute <name>` and `/hsc unmute <name>`
- Optional `[HH:MM]` timestamps
- Config menu with settings search, tooltips, HSV color picker, presets and live previews
- Master switch: turn the mod off to restore vanilla chat behavior
- Client-side only, with a built-in update checker

## 🎯 The essentials

- **Less spam.** Keep, shorten, dim or hide messages. Preview changes as you go.
- **Easier conversations.** Switch between All, Local, Party and Guild. Search with Ctrl+F, right-click to copy.
- **Your colors.** Customize chat colors and prefixes. Item rarity colors are preserved in compact messages.

---

## 🎨 Custom Rules

Highlight words with your own colors and **bold**, *italic* or underline — or hide messages containing unwanted phrases. Choose the channel and optional case sensitivity.

**Example:** highlight `downtime` in Party chat → Anyone need **downtime**?

*Bold illustrates the highlight here; colors are customizable in-game.* Open **`/hsc` → Custom Rules** to get started.
